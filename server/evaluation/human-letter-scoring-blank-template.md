## Proposed score anchors

These are **PROPOSED** anchors for the pilot, not officially approved rubric rules. Use the evaluator-approved rubric addendum for any clarifications. Enter one integer from 0 to 15 for each category.

- **0:** No relevant evidence
- **1-3:** Barely mentioned
- **4-8:** Brief or vague
- **9-12:** Present with some specifics
- **13-15:** Detailed and specific

Do not reward letter length, repeated mentions, or keywords by themselves. Give a concise rationale for each score without quoting or reproducing letter text.
